How it's made

Our process

  1. Receiving

    Raw hides are sorted and the fat layer is removed and sold separately.

  2. Liming & fleshing

    Hides are limed, fleshed and divided into shoulder, bellies and croupon.

  3. Splitting

    Croupon is split at 3.2–3.3 mm to separate grain and split layers.

  4. Tanning

    Grain is tanned to wet blue, then retanned and dried to crust or finished as suede.
