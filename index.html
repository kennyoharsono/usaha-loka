Where leather meets legacy

PT Usaha Loka Indonesia tans bovine, sheep and goat skins for footwear, leather goods, furniture and automotive makers, with a tanning heritage in Malang that goes back to 1941.

Request samples See our leathers

Leather made the long way, at industrial scale

Since 1989 Usaha Loka has grown into one of the pioneers of the Indonesian leather industry. Our roots go back to 1941 and to a long tradition of vegetable tanning, including Vachetta.

In recent years we have invested heavily in modernising the factory, upgrading equipment and building the capacity to serve buyers around the world. Every stage, from splitting raw hides to the final spray finish, happens under our own roof and is checked in our own laboratory.

Four generations of leather in Malang

  1. 1941

    Kasin begins tanning sole leather for military boots, one of the first tanneries in Indonesia.

  2. 1970

    Chrome-tanned upper leather for shoes, bags and wallets joins the range.

  3. 1989

    PT Usaha Loka is founded and grows into a pioneer of Indonesian leather.

  4. Today

    A modernised factory, LWG Gold rated, supplying buyers worldwide.

Responsibility

LWGGold

Usaha Loka is rated Gold as a leather manufacturer by the Leather Working Group (audit PTJ101, valid to 5 June 2027), which audits tanneries on environmental performance, chemical management and traceability.

We hold ourselves to five commitments: sustainability, integrity and mutual respect, continual innovation, care for our people, and the highest standards of workmanship.
