Malang, East Java. Since 1941.

Tanned with bark, built to last

Kasin is one of Indonesia's oldest tanneries. For more than 80 years we have made vegetable-tanned leather for soles, belts, saddlery and fine leather goods.

Leathers

Four leathers, each made the way it has been made in Malang for decades.

  • Vegetable sole leather

    Our first product, made since 1941. Dense, firm and hard-wearing: it soled Indonesian military boots, and still serves shoemakers and industry, from conveyor belts to textile machine parts and pump valves.

Our history

  1. 1941

    Kasin starts tanning sole leather, supplying soles for military boots.

  2. 1970

    We begin making finished upper leather for shoe, handbag and leather goods makers.

  3. 80+

    Years of family values, service and quality.

  4. Today

    Part of PT Usaha Loka Indonesia, an LWG Gold rated tannery.
